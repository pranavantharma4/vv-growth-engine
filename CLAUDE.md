## THE OBSERVATION RULE — the most important rule, do not break
Cold outreach CANNOT see inside their ad account. So:
- The hook = a REAL, externally-visible fact (their offer, price, funnel, launch, waitlist, content) — something verifiable from their site/profile.
- The leak = framed as a HYPOTHESIS: "the common leak in this setup is X — the free audit shows if it's happening to you."
- NEVER imply you've already seen their account ("noticed your campaigns are leaking," "which campaign is bleeding"). That's the fatal mistake — sharp founders instantly clock it as a mail-merge and ignore it.
- NEVER state a private number (spend, ROAS, wasted $) you can't see. SKIP rather than invent.
